Industrial Prototyping and Redesign for Batch Production

If a working prototype is slow to build, or an idea needs to prove its performance before a batch order, split the project into two decisions: what the prototype must demonstrate and how the first batch will be accepted.

What to share for an initial review

Describe the intended function, the current design or prototype, the first-batch quantity and your target date. If a drawing, photo or model is available, only share information you are allowed to disclose in the first message.

Industrial prototyping

From prototype to first batch

Functional prototype

Agree on the essential performance and test conditions, such as load, throughput, accuracy or cycle time. Design and build the parts needed to run that test.

Redesign for manufacturing and assembly

Review difficult or expensive parts, critical tolerances and the assembly sequence so the design can be built repeatedly and inspected.

First-batch preparation

Depending on the agreed scope, prepare models and drawings, a parts list and acceptance criteria so the first batch can be built, inspected and used to inform the next order.
