Dual-Purpose Industrial CNC

A CNC for wood and light metals that becomes a large 3D printer with a head change.

Year 2023CNCCAMStructural design
Machine

An industrial CNC machine for cutting wood and light metals, with a large work table (about 100×100 cm) and a modular design: with a simple head change it becomes a high-capacity 3D printer.

The goal was an affordable multi-purpose machine for small and medium workshops.

My role

Structural design, drive and motor selection, and commissioning the G-code control system.

Key facts

Working envelopeabout 100×100×30 cm
MaterialsWood and light metals
FeatureInterchangeable head: CNC router or 3D printer
